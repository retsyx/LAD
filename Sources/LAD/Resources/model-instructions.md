lad_render retains the view presets isometric/front/back/top/bottom/left/right and also supports arbitrary cameras. Optional yawDegrees and elevationDegrees override the preset azimuth/elevation; yaw 0 places the camera along +X, yaw 90 along +Y, elevation 0 lies in XY, and +90/-90 look from above/below along Z. Optional rollDegrees rotates the image counterclockwise for positive values. cameraTarget is a CAD-world [x,y,z] point in millimetres, defaulting to the fitted bounds center of the visible design or solidIndices subset. zoom is a positive magnification multiplier relative to automatic fit: 1 keeps the fitted view, 2 doubles magnification, and 0.5 halves it. For example, {"view":"isometric","yawDegrees":25,"elevationDegrees":18,"rollDegrees":10,"zoom":1.5} gives a custom oblique close view. Alternatively, cameraPosition supplies an explicit CAD-world [x,y,z] base eye position looking at cameraTarget; do not combine it with yawDegrees or elevationDegrees. Perspective zoom divides the base eye-to-target distance by zoom, so zoom 1 preserves an explicit position exactly. Orthographic zoom (projection:"isometric") keeps the base eye position unchanged and divides the orthographic view scale by zoom; its base scale is the base eye-to-target distance times tan(38 degrees / 2). For example, {"cameraPosition":[80,-50,35],"cameraTarget":[10,5,2],"zoom":1} uses that exact camera pose. Roll and the magnification multiplier work in both projections. Coordinates must be finite and within +/-1000000 mm, elevation within [-90,90], and zoom within [0.000001,1000000]; coincident eye/target or conflicting camera controls are errors. Choose these options only when useful for the existing visual review; they do not require extra renders or change the user's viewport.
