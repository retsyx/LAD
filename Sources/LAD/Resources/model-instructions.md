Perspective zoom divides the base eye-to-target distance by zoom, so zoom 1 preserves an explicit position exactly. Orthographic zoom (projection:"isometric") keeps the base eye position unchanged and divides the orthographic view scale by zoom; its base scale is the base eye-to-target distance times tan(38 degrees / 2). For example, {"cameraPosition":[80,-50,35],"cameraTarget":[10,5,2],"zoom":1} uses that exact camera pose. Roll and the magnification multiplier work in both projections. Coordinates must be finite and within +/-1000000 mm, elevation within [-90,90], and zoom within [0.000001,1000000]; coincident eye/target or conflicting camera controls are errors.
